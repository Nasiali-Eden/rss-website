Making God’s Word accessible to every language community.

The need

South Sudan is home to diverse language communities, many of which have limited access to Scripture and literacy resources in their own languages.

Our response

Through our Bible Translation and Literacy programme, we support efforts to make God’s Word accessible, understandable and relevant to local communities.

What we do

  • Bible translation into South Sudanese languages
  • Mother-tongue literacy programmes
  • Scripture engagement
  • Audio Scripture resources
  • Digital Scripture resources
  • Translation capacity building
  • Literacy and reading initiatives
  • Culturally relevant Christian resources
