Partner with us

Transformation is stronger when we work together.

Relief South Sudan welcomes partnerships with churches, foundations, donors, humanitarian organisations, development agencies, businesses and individuals who share our commitment to serving vulnerable communities.

Five ways to partner

Funding

Support programmes that respond to critical community needs.

Technical Support

Share expertise, knowledge and skills that strengthen our programmes and institutional capacity.

Strategic Partnerships

Collaborate with us to design and implement sustainable community initiatives.

Prayer & Advocacy

Stand with communities through prayer and help raise awareness of their needs and opportunities.

Resources

Support the provision of learning materials, Scripture resources, water facilities, livelihood inputs and other essential resources.
