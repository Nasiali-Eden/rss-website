Faith-based relief & community development

Demonstrating the love of Christ through compassionate service.

Relief South Sudan serves vulnerable and underserved communities across South Sudan through Bible translation, trauma healing, leadership development, clean water, and the empowerment of women and girls.

Who we are

South Sudanese. Community-based. Christ-centred.

We are a South Sudanese, community-based Christian organisation. We believe lasting transformation happens when people are supported spiritually, socially, emotionally, educationally and economically — so we combine Christian ministry with practical action that restores dignity, hope and resilience.
