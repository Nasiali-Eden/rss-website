About us

Faith. Compassion. Dignity. Hope. Transformation.

Relief South Sudan (RSS) is a South Sudanese community-based, non-profit and Christian organisation committed to serving vulnerable and underserved communities through holistic ministry, humanitarian assistance and sustainable community development.

Who we are

Lasting transformation touches the whole person.

We believe that lasting transformation happens when people are supported spiritually, socially, emotionally, educationally and economically. Through our programmes, we combine Christian ministry with practical interventions that promote dignity, hope, resilience and sustainable development.

Our work focuses on reaching underserved and unreached communities, strengthening local leadership, empowering women and girls, improving access to essential services, promoting peace and healing, and making God’s Word accessible to every language community.

We work alongside churches, communities, government institutions, national and international organisations, faith-based organisations and development partners to create meaningful and lasting impact across South Sudan.

Vision

To transform communities across South Sudan where people have access to God’s Word and opportunities to live with dignity, hope, peace and sustainable development.

Mission

To reach underserved and unreached communities by combining Bible translation, literacy, humanitarian assistance, leadership development and community empowerment, demonstrating the love of Christ through compassionate service.

Accountability & registration

Relief South Sudan is registered with the Relief and Rehabilitation Commission (RRC), Eastern Equatoria State, as a local charitable organisation under Chapter 3, Section 10 of the South Sudan NGO Act 2016 (Registration No. 95). We are committed to serving communities with compassion, integrity, professionalism and accountability.
